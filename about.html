About these concepts

Three early directions for a new Cadorath homepage. They show what's possible. They don't lock anything in.

Each one tells the same story from a different angle: Cadorath brings back parts that would otherwise be scrapped, and holds the approvals to prove it.

The finished site comes out of a full strategy and creative deep dive with your team. That's where we agree who the site is for, what it needs to say first, and how to show your real work. The final design will be sharper, more specific to Cadorath, and built on what we learn together.

What's real and what's placeholder

  • Approvals, certificate numbers and capabilities come from Cadorath's own published information.
  • Part searches and quote forms run on sample data. Nothing is sent.
  • Some photography is licensed stand-in imagery. Real photos of your people and shop floor replace it.

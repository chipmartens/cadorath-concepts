01 Worn

The manual says replace it.

A new casting can take months. Scrap and buy new is the default answer for a worn housing, and the aircraft waits. A Cadorath repair starts with the part you already own.

02 Inspected

We look closely before anyone says scrap.

Fluorescent penetrant, magnetic particle, ultrasonic and eddy current inspection, CMM measurement and a metallurgy lab. When the manual has no repair, our engineers write one and approve it under our own Transport Canada design delegation, DAO 15-C-01.

03 Plated

Plating is where we started, in 1954.

The machined faces get their finish in our own Winnipeg shops. Twenty plating and anodizing processes, from hard chrome to zinc-nickel.

04 In service

Released with paperwork an auditor knows.

Approved by Transport Canada, the FAA and EASA, and inside the Airbus Helicopters and Rolls-Royce networks. The paperwork travels with the part.

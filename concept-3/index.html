We write the repair, prove it in our lab and do it in our own shops.

Transport Canada has delegated design approval to our engineers since 2015, under DAO 15‑C‑01, and we hold over one thousand Transport Canada approved repairs across rotor wing aircraft and power plants. A metallurgy and plating lab backs the engineering, with bond testing witnessed by our delegated approval staff. Then the part runs through our own shops: twenty plating and anodizing processes, HVOF, plasma and cold spray, vacuum brazing and heat treatment, liquid penetrant, magnetic particle, ultrasonic and eddy current inspection, CMM, and an engine test cell.
